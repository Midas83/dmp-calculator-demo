Credit card debt is the most frequent reason employees call an Evenkeel coach. This calculator compares your payments now with a nonprofit debt management plan.

What employees get

Each employee starts with a 30-minute call. The coach makes a plan with them and stays with them until it is done.

A coach who knows their name
One certified financial coach for each employee, by phone, video, or text. Coaches earn no commission and sell no products.
Emergency savings from each paycheck
Employees set an amount, and payroll moves it to a savings account in their name. They can take the money out at any time.
A way out of credit card debt
Coaches connect employees with a nonprofit credit counseling agency. A debt management plan can lower interest rates and set one monthly payment.
Help with the large decisions
A first home, student loans, a new baby, a parent who needs care. The coach gives clear steps for each one.

How it works for employers

Your team does three things. We do the remainder.

  1. Connect payroll

    We connect to your payroll system for eligibility and savings deductions. Your IT team approves one data connection.

  2. Tell your people

    We supply the launch emails, the break-room posters, and a 20-minute session for managers. Employees sign up on their phones.

  3. See the results

    Each quarter you get a report on sign-ups, savings balances, and debt paid off. The report shows totals only, not individuals.

Questions employers ask

Does the employer see an employee's finances?

No. Employers get totals for the full workforce. Coach conversations, calculator entries, and account balances stay private.

Who provides the debt management plans?

A nonprofit credit counseling agency provides the plans. Its certified counselors review each employee's cards and budget in a free call. Evenkeel does not receive a fee for a referral.

What does Evenkeel cost?

Employers pay a fixed monthly price for each eligible employee. Coaching and the savings account are free for employees.

How long does launch take?

Most employers launch in four weeks. The payroll connection is the longest step.
